About Us

We are three friends from the same department, studying in the 3rd semester of Data Science at Pak Austria Institute. We have always loved creativity—art, writing, animation—and we noticed that writers and artists rarely get a combined platform.

This idea came to our minds while remembering how some artworks take years to become masterpieces. Just like that, we also wanted to provide a platform where writers and artists can collaborate, innovate, and create animations or creative projects together.
