Art Weaver Rules & Guidelines

1️⃣ Account & Profile
Every user must create an account with a valid username and email.
Profiles should include accurate information and indicate the user’s role (Writer/Artist).

2️⃣ Content Creation
Writers: Can post stories, poems, blogs, and articles.
Artists: Can share paintings, sketches, illustrations, and digital art.
Every post must include a title and description.
Content must be original and free from plagiarism.

3️⃣ Community Conduct
All users must maintain respect and professionalism.
Hate speech, abusive language, discrimination, or harassment is strictly prohibited.

4️⃣ Posting & Sharing
Users retain ownership of their own posts.
Proper credit must be given if referencing someone else’s work.

5️⃣ Interaction & Collaboration
Users can collaborate on projects.
Respect deadlines and agreements in collaborations.
